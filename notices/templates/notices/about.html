About the College Digital Notice Board

This notice board helps the college community find current announcements without searching through multiple channels. Students can browse published notices, search by keyword or category, read full details and download attached PDFs.

Authorized college staff manage notices through a secure dashboard. Notices can be marked important, scheduled to expire, and published or kept as drafts.

WHAT YOU CAN FIND

  • Events, sports and placement news
  • Admissions and scholarship notices
  • Holidays, timetables and general updates
